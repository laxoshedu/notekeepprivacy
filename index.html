Welcome to NoteKeep. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how NoteKeep operates, how your data is processed, and why your note-taking environment remains private and secure.

Key Summary: NoteKeep is designed with a local-first privacy approach. All your handwritten notes, text documents, custom canvas drawings, and inserted media remain stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or sell your private documents to remote external servers.

1. Information Collection & Storage

NoteKeep operates locally on your client device using secure web storage engines (such as IndexedDB and Local Storage):

  • Note Content & Drawings: Your handwritten canvas paths, formatted text notes, diagrams, folder structures, and background paper presets are stored directly inside your device's browser/application database.
  • Uploaded Images & Presets: Any images or media inserted into your canvas or text documents are kept entirely on your device.
  • No Centralized Cloud Storage: NoteKeep does not automatically transmit, upload, or synchronize your private notes to any external database hosted by laxoshedu.

2. Device Permissions & Usage

To provide full studio functionality, NoteKeep may request access to certain client capabilities:

  • Storage & Media Access: Required when you choose to import background images into pages or export documents as PDF, JPEG, or image files.
  • Local Font API: Used strictly to allow you to apply locally installed system fonts to your rich text notes. Font data is accessed on-device and is never shared.

3. Data Exports & Sharing

All note exports (including PDF generation and JPEG image stitching) are processed locally on your device using client-side libraries. Any sharing of exported documents is solely initiated and managed by you through your operating system's standard share channels.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

NoteKeep does not integrate third-party tracking software, advertising telemetry, or behavioral profiling tools that collect your document content. Standard platform-level analytics (such as anonymous crash reports provided directly by Google Play Services) may be collected to ensure app stability and performance upgrades.

5. Children's Privacy

NoteKeep does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. Because all notes remain local on the user's device, the app provides a secure learning environment suitable for classroom, academic, and personal use.

6. Data Control & Deletion

You maintain 100% ownership and control over your notes:

  • Deleting Content: You can delete individual pages, text notes, or entire folders within the app at any time.
  • Clearing App Data: Uninstalling the application or clearing local application storage permanently removes all saved notes from your device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted directly within this page with an updated effective date.
